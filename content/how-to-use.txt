Choose a visualization from the menu on the left.
Press Play to start the construction animation.
Press Pause to stop the animation.
Press Reset to return the visualization to the beginning.
Use the Animation speed slider to make the animation slower or faster.
Use Show vertices to show or hide the vertices.
Use Show construction path to show or hide the edges and construction path.
Use the fullscreen button in the upper-right corner of the visualization to open a larger view.
Select a Rotate option to view a rotating projection of the hypercube.
