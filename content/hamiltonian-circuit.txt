Main heading = Hamiltonian Circuit
About heading =
A **Hamiltonian circuit** is a closed path that visits every vertex of a graph exactly once before returning to the starting vertex. This animation shows 16 dancers (represented by colored dots) each tracing out a Hamiltonian circuit over the 
hypercube and returning to their starting points at the end. The Hamiltonian circuit shown here has been implemented using 
the reflected Gray code familiar to computer scientists.
