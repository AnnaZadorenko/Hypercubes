A 4D hypercube is called a tesseract.

It can be imagined as two ordinary 3D cubes whose vertices are connected together by edges along the fourth dimension.

It has 16 vertices, 32 edges, 24 square faces and 8 3D cubes known as "cells" for its outer boundary.


FORMULAS

Vertices
V(n) = 2ⁿ

Edges
E(n) = n × 2ⁿ⁻¹


CURRENT DIMENSION: 4

Vertices = 2⁴ = 16

Edges = 4 × 2³ = 32
