This animation shows how a 4-cube (also known as a tesseract or hypercube) can be built up through the chain: point --> line --> square --> cube --> tesseract, if the motion of each object in this chain takes place in the dimension perpendicular to it. The animation is shown as it appears when projected from the four-dimensional space in which it takes place to the the two-dimensional plane of the computer screen.
The number of vertices of an n-cube is V(n) = 2ⁿ and the number of edges is E(n) = n × 2ⁿ⁻¹. The numbers for a tesseract, for which n - 4, are shown in the boxes below.
