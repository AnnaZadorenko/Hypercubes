About heading =
This animation shows a ballet performed on the skeleton of a tesseract (or hypercube).

The ballet begins with 16 dancers taking up positions at the vertices of the hypercube. At each step, the dancers EITHER approach each other along the edges of the hypercube to kiss at the middle and return to their starting points OR move along the edges of the hypercube to take up positions at other vertices. The first type of movement will be termed a kiss and the second a shuffle.

By a suitable combination of kisses and shuffles, each dancer kisses all the others just once and returns to his/her starting point at the beginning of the dance.  

As an encore, the dancers walk along the edges of the hypercube to visit all the vertices once before returning to their starting points (in other words, they trace out a Hamiltonian circuit on the hypercube).
