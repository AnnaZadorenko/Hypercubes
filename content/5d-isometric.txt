This animation shows how a 5-cube can be built up through the chain: point --> line --> square --> cube --> tesseract --> 5-cube, if each object in the chain is moved along one of the dimensions perpendicular to it. The animation is shown as it appears when projected from the five-dimensional space in which it occurs to the two-dimensional plane of the computer screen. Although the 5-cube has 32 vertices, they project into just the 31 points shown in the projection because two diamterically opposite vertices of the 5-cube go into the point at the center, which is shown as having twice the size of the other points to account for this effect. All the 80 edges of the 5-cube are visible as distinct line segments in this projection.
