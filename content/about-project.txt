The Hypercube Explorer is an interactive website designed to help users visualize hypercubes in various dimensions. It shows how higher-dimensional cubes can be represented through two-dimensional projections and animations.
The project includes visualizations of a 3-cube, 4-cube (or tesseract), 5-cube and 6-cube. Each visualization is built step by step so users can see how the vertices and edges of a lower dimensional cube glide through a new dimension to give rise to the vertices and edges of the next cube in the chain, until the final d-cube results. 
Users can control the animation, change its speed, show or hide vertices and the construction path, and view the visualization in fullscreen. Information about each hypercube is also provided, including its number of dimensions, vertices, edges, and the formulas used to calculate them.
The goal of this project is to make higher-dimensional geometry easier to explore and understand through interactive visualization.
