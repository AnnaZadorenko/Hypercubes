The Hypercube Explorer is an interactive website designed to help users visualize hypercubes in anumber of dimensions. It shows how higher-dimensional cubes can be visualized through two-dimensional projections and animations.

The project includes visualizations of a 3-cube, 4-cube (or tesseract), 5-cube and 6-cube. Each visualization is built up step by step so that users can see how the vertices and edges of a lower dimensional cube glide through a new dimension to give rise to the vertices and edges of the next cube in the chain, until the d-cube finally appears. 

Users can control the animation by changing its speed, showing or hiding vertices and/or the construction path, and viewing the animation in fullscreen. Information about each hypercube is also provided, including its dimension and the number of its vertices and edges. Click on "More about the d-cube" in the sidebar to learn how these numbers and other characteristics of the hypercube can be worked out. The sources listed under "References" in the sidebar also provide more information about
hypercubes in general. 

The goal of this project is to make higher-dimensional geometry easier for a lay audience to understand through a number of interactive visualizations.
