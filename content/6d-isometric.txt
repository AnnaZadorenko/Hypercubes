This animation shows how a 6-cube can be built up through the chain: point --> line --> square --> cube --> tesseract --> 5-cube --> 6-cube, if each object in the chain is moved along one of the dimensions perpendicular to it. The animation is shown as it appears when projected from the six-dimensional space in which it occurs to the two-dimensional plane of the computer screen. Although the 6-cube has 64 vertices, they go into just the 49 points shown in the projection because four of the vertices go into the point at the center of the projection whereas pairs of vertices go into the points in the second ring around the center. The points representing the doubled or quadrupled vertices are shown as twice or four times as large as the points representing lone vertices.
