This animation shows how a 3-cube (or ordinary three-dimensional cube) can be built up from a point that moves to give a line, with the line then giving rise to a square and the square to a cube. The motion of each object (point, line or square) takes place in the dimension perpendicular to it to give the next object in the chain. The animation shows how the process appears when projected on to the two-dimensional plane of the computer screen.
