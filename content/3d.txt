A 3-cube is the familiar three-dimensional version of a hypercube. This animation shows how it can be built from a point that moves to give a line, with the line then moving to give a square and the square to give a cube. The motion of each object (point, line or square) takes place in the dimension perpendicular to its own to give the next object in this chain. The animation shows how this process appears when it is projected on to the plane of the computer screen.
